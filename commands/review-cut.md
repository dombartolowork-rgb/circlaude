Use the `video-review` skill.

Build the checklist of what was asked for first (amends thread, WhatsApp, Vimeo
comments, Fathom transcript, the brief), then get the cut and the previous
version, run `circle vid-review`, and look at the frames before judging.

Report in chat: one row per amend with a verdict and timecode, then anything
changed that nobody asked for, then anything else found. Then ask where the
findings should go (email draft to the editor, Word report in the job folder,
Vimeo comment, WhatsApp, or nowhere). Send or post nothing without their go.
