# House style

## Never use em dashes

Not in email, documents, decks, LinkedIn copy, code comments or commit messages.
The character to avoid is `—`. Also avoid `–` (en dash) in prose.

Use instead, whichever fits:

- a comma, for a parenthetical aside
- brackets, for a true aside
- a colon, where the second half explains the first
- a full stop and a new sentence, where the break is a real one
- a spaced hyphen ` - `, which is the house style for an abrupt break

Ordinary hyphens inside words are fine: day-to-day, hands-on, non-developer.

**Why:** em dashes read as AI-written, and they are not how we write.

## Email

**Draft first, show the text, send on the user's say-so.** People revise framing once they
see it. Wait for an explicit "send it".

**Separate personalised emails** beat one group email when the same message goes
to several people. Write each to that person by name.

**Delete superseded drafts** so Drafts does not fill with junk.

Plain styling. No blue text. No signature block beyond their normal one.

## Documents and decks

Ramp the reader: **setup before ideas, evidence and financials before
conclusions, topline before detail**, and a transition line between sections.

Circle values **cross-document consistency**, so match the register and structure of
the document before it rather than inventing a new shape.

## Attribution

Attribute credit accurately when several people did the work, by name and by what
they actually did. Get this right.

## Numbers

Round for speech and voiceover ("221 thousand", "8,100 samples", "1,300 souls").
Keep them exact in finance documents. Client data needs client sign-off before it
is published anywhere public.
